# Calibration of iron, folate and calcium estimates

14,526 recipes matched to 220 USDA prepared dishes; correction fitted on half the dishes, tested on the other half. Bias = estimate / USDA (1.00 = unbiased); top 20% = the highest estimates, which an optimiser picks.

| Nutrient | Within 2× before → after | Median bias before → after | Top-20% bias before → after | a | b |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Iron | 65% → 81% | 1.21 → 0.84 | 3.21 → 0.85 | -7.13 | 0.10 |
| Folate | 49% → 73% | 1.40 → 0.90 | 2.87 → 1.13 | -2.11 | 0.37 |
| Calcium | 42% → 52% | 1.92 → 0.96 | 3.36 → 0.55 | -2.46 | 0.23 |
